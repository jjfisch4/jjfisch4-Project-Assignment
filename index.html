Welcome to altermore

New clothes are expensive, and sometimes nothing in the store looks like what you want. I started altering, upcycling, and making my own clothes because I didn't have much money and I wanted a creative outlet.

This site shows what I have made, what I bought to make it, how long it took, and the videos that helped me when I got stuck. If you want something that is truly yours, I hope this inspires you to go out and make it.
