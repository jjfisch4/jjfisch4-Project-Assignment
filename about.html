About altermore

Altering, upcycling, and creating clothes on a small budget.

How it started

I didn't like most of the clothes I was seeing in stores, I didn't have many materials, and I didn't have much money. Buying something new that looked like what I actually wanted was either impossible or too expensive.

So I started changing what I already had. Altering a shirt turned into painting one, painting turned into rebuilding a pair of shoes, and what began as a way to save money became my creative outlet.

My workstation in the after finishing a project.

Why I make my own clothes

Clothes are one of the easiest ways to show who you are, and it's hard to do that when everyone is wearing the same thing. Making my own means every piece is something nobody else has, and it costs far less than buying something similar.

I also want to show how approachable this is. You don't need an expensive machine or years of experience. Most of my projects started with a basic shirt, some paint, and a few hours.

Where I find inspiration

Most of my ideas come from music and the art that goes with it. My first shirt was inspired by Starchild by Sweetpill and my second by Soup by bugsy. I like picking an album, song, or image that means something to me and turning it into something I can wear.

When I get stuck on a technique, I look up a tutorial. I link the videos that helped me on each project page so you can use them too.
