Shirt 1

A painted shirt inspired by Starchild by Sweetpill.

The finished shirt.

The idea

This was my first painted shirt. I wanted a design that felt hand-made instead of printed, so I painted it with loose, visible brushstrokes: a yellow swirl with red eyes, outlined in blue.

Cost: $22

Time: 3 hours

Type: Painted design on a store-bought shirt

Inspiration: Starchild by Sweetpill

The inspiration

Starchild by Sweetpill, the inspiration for this shirt.

This shirt started with Starchild by Sweetpill. I wanted to take what I liked about it and turn it into something I could wear.

My version isn't an exact copy. I painted it in my own loose, hand-made style.

What I used

Here is everything I bought for this project. My honest thoughts on each item are on the Materials page.

Item What I used it for Where to buy
Old Navy t-shirt The base for the design Old Navy
Carbon tracing paper Transferring the sketch onto the shirt Amazon
16-color matte acrylic paint pack Painting the design Michaels
FolkArt textile medium Mixed into the acrylic paint so it works on fabric Michaels

How I made it

  1. Prepare Paint. Mix your paints with the fabric medium, one part paint two parts medium.
  2. Sketch the design. I drew the design on paper at the size I wanted it on the shirt.
  3. Transfer it. I placed the carbon tracing paper on the shirt and traced over the sketch to leave an outline.
  4. Paint the main shapes. I filled in the yellow swirl first, working in loose strokes.
  5. Add the details. I painted the red eyes and the blue outline last, once the yellow had dried.
  6. Let it dry. Let the paint dry completely before folding or wearing the shirt.
  7. Heat Treatement. Once the paint is dry, grab an extra piece of fabric, I use a pillowcase. Place it over the design, grab an iron and make sure it does not steam. Iron on top of the fabric for a few minutes, then its ready to wear and wash.
